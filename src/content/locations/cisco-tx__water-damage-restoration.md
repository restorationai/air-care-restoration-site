## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Cisco

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mount and portable units before it has a chance to wick further into subfloor or wall cavities. In Cisco's older downtown buildings, where plaster and solid-sawn lumber framing are still common, that wicking happens slower than in modern drywall construction but travels further once it starts, so our moisture mapping goes beyond the visibly wet area. On newer slab homes, we're watching for moisture trapped between the slab and flooring materials like vinyl plank or tile, which can trap water against the concrete and slow drying considerably.
