Renovating a home in Winters often starts with a problem nobody planned for: a cracked slab from shifting clay, a kitchen that still carries smoke odor from a stove fire, or a bathroom that never fully dried out after a supply line failure. Air Care Restoration handles the renovation, remodel, and general contracting work that follows those events, as well as standalone kitchen and bathroom updates for homeowners who are ready for a change without the damage.

## Why Winters Homes Need Careful Renovation Planning

Winters sits in Runnels County, in the Big Country region south of Abilene, where the soil is predominantly expansive clay. That clay swells during wet spells and contracts hard during the long dry summers, which puts steady pressure on slab foundations and the plumbing that runs through them. A lot of the housing stock here dates to the postwar decades, meaning original cast iron or galvanized supply lines that are well past their design life. When one of those lines fails inside a wall or under a slab, the renovation isn't just cosmetic. It usually involves opening flooring or drywall, replacing a section of pipe, and rebuilding the finish work around it, not just painting over a water stain.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Winters

We start with a walkthrough to separate what's structural from what's finish-level, because those two categories get priced and scheduled differently. If the project follows storm, fire, or water damage, we document the loss first for the insurance file, then move into demolition of compromised material down to sound substrate. From there, framing and rough-in work (electrical, plumbing, HVAC) happens before any drywall goes back up, and we coordinate permitting through the county's building office before work that touches structural or utility systems begins. Finish selections for kitchens and bathrooms, cabinetry, countertops, tile, and fixtures, get locked in during the rough-in phase so there's no delay waiting on materials once we're ready to close up walls.

## Reaching Winters from Abilene

Our crews travel to Winters from our Abilene base, generally by US-83/277, which keeps the drive to roughly 40 minutes under normal conditions. Because we're a 24/7 operation, we're able to schedule initial assessments and urgent board-up or mitigation work outside standard business hours when a renovation project starts with active damage, then move into the planned rebuild phase on a schedule that works around the homeowner's routine.

## Insurance Coordination for Rebuild Projects

When a remodel is triggered by a covered loss, fire, a burst pipe, storm wind damage, the insurance side of the job runs in parallel with construction. We photograph and document existing conditions before demolition, keep a materials and labor log as the rebuild progresses, and submit that documentation to the carrier so supplements for unforeseen damage, like rot discovered once drywall comes down, don't stall the timeline. For renovations that aren't insurance-related, like a straightforward kitchen or bathroom update, we scope and price the job directly with the homeowner without that extra documentation layer.

## Local note

A detail that catches a lot of homeowners off guard here: because so many Winters properties sit on slab foundations over reactive clay, a bathroom remodel that includes moving a drain line often needs a foundation contractor's input before we cut the slab, not after. Skipping that step is how a straightforward remodel turns into a foundation repair mid-project. We flag this during the initial walkthrough rather than discovering it once demo is underway.
