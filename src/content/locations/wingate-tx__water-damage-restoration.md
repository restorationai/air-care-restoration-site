## Why Wingate Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of the housing stock around Wingate is older frame construction built on pier-and-beam foundations, which handles ground moisture differently than a slab. Water from a plumbing failure or a hard rain doesn't just pool on a slab surface, it migrates under the house into the crawlspace, where it can sit against joists and ductwork for weeks before anyone notices a musty smell upstairs. Clay-heavy soil common across this part of Texas also swells and contracts with seasonal moisture swings, which stresses older foundations and plumbing joints and is a quiet contributor to slow leaks that go undetected until a floor starts to feel soft underfoot.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Wingate

On arrival, our crew identifies the water source and shuts it off if it hasn't been already, then moves into extraction using truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on how the property is set up. For pier-and-beam homes, that often means getting into the crawlspace directly rather than just pulling moisture from the living space above. We take moisture readings in framing, subfloor, and drywall, and set containment and air movers to dry the structure in place following the IICRC S500 drying standard rather than guessing when it looks dry. Because well water carries different mineral and bacterial content than treated municipal supply, we also flag any water source contamination concerns early so antimicrobial treatment decisions are made correctly rather than assumed.
