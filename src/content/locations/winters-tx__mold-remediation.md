**Mold remediation in Winters** often starts with a conversation about well water or an old window unit, not a dramatic flood. In this part of Runnels County, mold problems tend to creep in slowly, through a slow roof leak after a hailstorm, a crawlspace that never quite dries out, or an evaporative cooler that's been pumping moisture into attic insulation for years. By the time the smell reaches the living room, the colony behind the wall or under the flooring has usually had a head start.

## Why Winters Properties See Mold Issues

Winters sits in a stretch of West Texas where the climate swings hard: dry heat for most of the year, then sudden heavy rain events that overwhelm older drainage and roofing systems built decades before current standards. A lot of the housing stock here dates back to the mid-20th century, with pier-and-beam foundations and crawlspaces that were never built with vapor barriers. That combination, humidity trapped under the house with no real place to escape, is one of the most common mold triggers we see on inspections in and around town. Swamp coolers, still common on older homes in this region, add another layer: they're efficient in dry heat but can push moisture into attic spaces and wall cavities if not properly maintained, creating the exact conditions mold needs to establish itself.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Winters

We start with a visual and moisture inspection to find where the colony is actually rooted, not just where it's visible, since drywall and plaster can look fine on the surface while the stud bay behind it stays damp. Once we've mapped the extent of the growth, we set up containment, usually negative-air plastic barriers with HEPA filtration, so spores don't spread to unaffected rooms during removal. Affected porous materials like drywall, insulation, and in some cases subflooring get removed and bagged for disposal rather than treated in place, since mold on porous surfaces can't be reliably cleaned out. Non-porous surfaces get HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an antimicrobial, and we run air scrubbers through the containment zone before we close the job out. For pier-and-beam homes with crawlspace moisture, we also address the source, whether that's a vapor barrier gap, a plumbing leak, or poor exterior drainage, because remediation without fixing the moisture source just buys the mold a few more months before it's back.

## Winters Insurance & HOA Coordination

Mold claims in this part of Texas tend to hinge on whether the moisture source was sudden and accidental, like a burst supply line, or gradual, like a long-term roof leak or crawlspace condensation, since carriers handle those very differently. We document moisture readings, photograph the affected materials before removal, and provide a written scope of work that most adjusters can use directly, which helps avoid back-and-forth delays on a claim. Winters doesn't have the dense HOA presence you'd find in a larger metro, but for any property under a homeowners association or a rental management company, we'll work directly with that third party to keep approvals and documentation moving.

## Local note

A lot of the older homes we see around Winters and the surrounding farm-to-market roads still rely on window units or swamp coolers instead of central HVAC, and those systems can hide a surprising amount of condensation damage inside the wall cavity directly behind the unit. We always check that wall section specifically during mold inspections in homes with older cooling setups, even when the homeowner's complaint is about a completely different room.
