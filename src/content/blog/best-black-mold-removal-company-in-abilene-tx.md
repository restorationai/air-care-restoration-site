## What to Ask Before You Hire Anyone on This List

Ask for the state mold remediation license number and check it against the [TDLR license lookup tool](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/LicenseSearch/) before any work starts. Ask whether the remediation plan follows the IICRC S520 containment and clearance-testing process, and ask for a written scope of work, not a verbal estimate, before signing anything.

If your loss came from a sudden, accidental water event like a burst pipe, check your homeowners policy. Mold coverage is often limited or capped, and typically applies only when the mold traces back to a covered sudden loss, according to the [Texas Department of Insurance](https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/when-are-water-damage-and-mold-covered-by-insurance.html).
