## Restoration emergencies common in Stamford

Stamford sits in Jones County, West Texas, where long stretches of heat and drought are regularly broken by sudden, heavy thunderstorms that can dump more rain in an hour than the ground or the town's older drainage can absorb. That swing between bone-dry and saturated is hard on foundations and roofs alike: clay-heavy soils common across this part of the state expand and contract with moisture changes, which stresses slab foundations and can open gaps around plumbing penetrations and exterior walls. Add in a housing stock where many homes date back several decades, and you get a steady pattern of slow leaks behind walls, AC condensation lines backing up in summer, and the occasional frozen or burst pipe during a hard winter cold snap. Wind-driven storms also bring roof damage that, if not tarped quickly, turns into a much bigger water intrusion problem.
