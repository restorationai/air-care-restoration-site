## Restoration emergencies common in Blackwell

Blackwell sits in a part of West Texas where weather swings hard between extremes, and that's usually what drives the calls we get. Summer thunderstorms can dump heavy rain in a short window, and on older roofs or around aging flashing that means water finds its way into attics and wall cavities fast. Hail is a regular visitor to this part of the state, and a damaged roof that isn't tarped quickly turns into a ceiling and insulation problem within a day or two. Winter brings occasional hard freezes that catch exposed or poorly insulated pipe runs off guard, and a burst line in a slab home can saturate flooring and drywall long before anyone notices standing water. Extended dry stretches between storms also mean foundations shift and settle, which opens up gaps around slabs and plumbing penetrations that let moisture in quietly over time.
